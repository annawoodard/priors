# Log

Append-only. One entry per ingest, review, query, mentor session or reading
session:

`## YYYY-MM-DD <kind> | <subject>` followed by a few lines: what was done,
what changed, and for reading sessions what the owner read.

Kinds: `ingest`, `review`, `query`, `mentor`, `read`, and, once they're
built, `digest` and `check`.
